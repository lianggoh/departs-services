Coverage details

Warranty

Every system is stress-tested (CPU and GPU load) before it ships. This catches almost everything, but intermittent RAM or board faults can occasionally slip past initial testing, which is exactly what the 30-day window covers.

Coverage First 30 days After 30 days (lifetime)
Labor Free. All repairs and replacements covered at no charge Free permanently if the issue stems from my workmanship
Parts Free. Replaced at no charge for defects or assembly faults Billed at cost, or covered under manufacturer warranty if applicable. I manage the claim on your behalf
Other Not covered: wear, aging, and unrelated failures.
Shipping Shipping damage or missing parts must be reported within 24 hours of delivery.
Not covered: software and firmware, RGB and lighting, normal wear, or anything after physical modification, overclocking beyond validated settings, unauthorized part removal, or software changes that cause instability.
Not sure if a change affects your warranty? Text me first.
Beyond the warranty window: for workstation builds I'll still make reasonable efforts to source replacements or work with manufacturers on your behalf. No promises, but I don't disappear after 30 days.
Policy

Cancellation & Returns

All sales are final once your system is delivered. Custom builds can't go back into inventory as new, so returns aren't accepted after handoff. DeParts isn't responsible for data loss or corruption. Back up anything important before delivery.

  • Before order Full deposit refund.
  • After parts ordered 50% deposit is non-refundable. If components are still sealed and assembly hasn't started, a partial refund may be issued minus a 15–20% restocking and price-adjustment fee.
  • After assembly starts Deposit is fully non-refundable.
