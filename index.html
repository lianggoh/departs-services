Client case study
Consulting engagement
Bay Area AI researcher — LLM inference platform
Situation Client planned a Mac Pro Ultra 512GB at ~$15K for running the largest available open-source LLMs locally.
Finding Unified RAM delivers 800 GB/s vs 1,792 GB/s on dedicated VRAM — no CUDA support locks out vLLM, TensorRT-LLM, and fine-tuning entirely.
Outcome Redirected to 2× RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell — 4–6× faster inference at the same price. Client avoided a fundamental architecture mistake before spending.
