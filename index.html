Client case study
Consulting engagement

Situation
Client planned a Mac Pro M5 Max 128GB at ~$5.5K for running the largest available open-source LLMs locally.
Finding
Mac unified RAM runs 70B models at ~8–15 tok/s — slower than you read — while dedicated VRAM hits 34–90 tok/s with full CUDA support.
Outcome
Redirected to 1-2× RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell — 4–6× faster inference. Fundamental architecture mistake avoided before spending.
